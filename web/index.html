Connect Chrome

Dustpan reaches your Google accounts through the Dustpan bridge extension. Each Chrome profile with the extension shows up in the Chrome profile menu above.

  1. Open chrome://extensions in each Chrome profile you want to clean and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Click Load unpacked and choose dustpan/extensions/chrome.
